FAQ

Questions we often get

What rental options do you offer?

We offer self-drive car rental and car rental with a driver. The option available can differ per vehicle, so please tell us which vehicle you are interested in and we will confirm what is possible.

Do you deliver to the airport?

Yes, airport delivery is one of our services. Delivery details and timing are arranged in advance based on your flight schedule and our availability. Contact us on WhatsApp to arrange it.

Can you deliver the car to my hotel or villa?

Hotel and villa delivery can be arranged, depending on your location and our availability. Send us your accommodation details and dates so we can confirm.

Is every vehicle always available?

Not always. Our fleet combines our own vehicles with partner vehicles, so availability changes from day to day. Please contact us to check the current availability for your travel dates.

Do you have premium vehicles such as the Alphard?

Yes, our fleet includes premium models such as the New Alphard, along with the Hiace Premio / Luxury for larger groups. As these units are limited, please ask us in advance about availability.

Can foreign tourists rent a car for self-drive?

Self-drive rental for international visitors may be subject to requirements such as a valid driving licence and other documents. Requirements can vary, so please contact us on WhatsApp and we will explain what is needed for your situation.

How do I check prices and rental terms?

We do not publish rates online because they depend on the vehicle, duration and delivery arrangement. Send us a WhatsApp message with your dates and preferred car, and we will reply with the details.

Ready to explore Bali?

Tell us your travel dates and the car you have in mind. We will check availability and get back to you on WhatsApp.
